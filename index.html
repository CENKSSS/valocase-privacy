ValoCase Privacy Policy

Developer: Sahara Games

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sahara Games ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use ValoCase ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and profile data — a device-generated guest account identifier, internal user identifier, chosen display name, and selected avatar, stored on our servers so your progress persists across sessions.
  • Device and usage data — device model, operating system version, app version, language, and general usage or diagnostic data, such as crash logs, used to operate, secure, and improve the App.
  • Advertising identifiers — a resettable device advertising ID, which may be collected by our advertising partner when ads are shown or watched.

We do not intentionally collect your real name, phone number, precise location, full payment card details, or banking information.

2. Gameplay Data

We collect and store gameplay-related data including your in-app currency balance, inventory contents, virtual item ownership, case-opening history, mission progress, rewards, battle results, rewarded ad status, purchase delivery status, and progression stats. This data is used to operate the App's features, save progress, prevent abuse, and improve the game experience.

3. AdMob Rewarded Ads

ValoCase shows optional rewarded video ads, such as ads used to earn bonus in-app currency, through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and similar data to serve ads, measure performance, limit abuse, and, where consent is provided, personalize ads.

You are never required to watch an ad to use the core features of the App. Google's use of advertising data is governed by the Google Ads Partner Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent

Where required by applicable law, including GDPR in the EEA/UK and similar regulations elsewhere, we may request your consent before showing personalized ads or processing non-essential advertising-related data.

ValoCase may use Google's User Messaging Platform to request, manage, and store advertising-related consent choices. Depending on your region and applicable law, you may be shown a consent form before certain advertising-related data processing occurs.

  • You may choose non-personalized ads where offered by the consent prompt.
  • You may reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • You may manage certain advertising preferences through your device settings, Google account settings, or consent choices where available.

5. In-App Purchases

ValoCase may offer optional in-app purchases of virtual currency and virtual items through Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. The App does not directly collect, process, or store your full payment card number, banking details, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Purchase-related information, such as purchased virtual currency, purchased virtual items, purchase delivery status, and transaction-related identifiers, may be stored by ValoCase to deliver purchased content, restore purchases where applicable, prevent abuse, and provide support.

6. Virtual Currency and Virtual Items

ValoCase uses virtual currency, virtual cases, virtual skins, rewards, missions, battles, and other in-game items. All in-app currency and virtual items are for entertainment purposes only.

Virtual currency and virtual items have no real-world monetary value and cannot be cashed out, sold, traded, transferred, redeemed, or exchanged for real money, real-world goods, or real-world services, whether inside or outside the App.

7. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following categories of third-party services to operate the App:

  • Advertising — Google AdMob, used for rewarded ads and ad measurement.
  • Consent management — Google User Messaging Platform, used to request and manage advertising consent where required.
  • Backend hosting — our cloud application server and database providers, used to store the account, profile, gameplay, inventory, wallet, and progress data described above.
  • App distribution and purchases — Google Play, used for app delivery, in-app purchases, app integrity, diagnostics, crash reporting, and platform services.
  • Game engine and related services — Unity services, where enabled for diagnostics, analytics, or platform functionality.

These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and according to their own terms.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share information with third-party service providers only as needed for them to provide the advertising, consent management, hosting, app distribution, purchase processing, diagnostics, security, or platform services described in this policy. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Sahara Games, our users, or others.

9. Data Storage and Security

Account and gameplay data is stored on our cloud application server and database providers. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect this data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as necessary to provide the App, maintain user progress, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms. Some data may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed.

Advertising identifiers and advertising-related data may be retained by our advertising partners according to their own retention policies. You may reset or limit your device advertising identifier through your device settings where available.

11. Children's Privacy

ValoCase is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required by your local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. User Data Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or to ask questions about how your data is processed. Some requests may require verification to protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with applicable law.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. The updated version will be posted on this page.
