ValoCase Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

ValoCase is a mobile game developed and operated by Sahara Games. This Privacy Policy explains how ValoCase collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the game.

ValoCase uses virtual currency, virtual cases, virtual skins, rewards, missions, battles, and other in-game items. All virtual currency and virtual items in ValoCase are for entertainment purposes only. They have no real-world monetary value and cannot be cashed out, sold, traded, transferred, redeemed, or exchanged for real money or real-world goods.

2. Information We Collect

ValoCase may collect and process gameplay-related data needed to operate the game. This may include:

  • Guest account identifier or internal user identifier
  • Virtual wallet balance
  • Inventory and virtual item ownership
  • Case opening results
  • Mission progress and rewards
  • Battle results and game activity
  • Rewarded advertisement status
  • Basic technical and diagnostic information related to game functionality

This information is used to provide game features, save and synchronize progress, prevent abuse or fraud, maintain game balance, troubleshoot issues, and improve the game experience.

3. Advertising

ValoCase uses Google AdMob to show advertisements, including rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements may allow users to receive in-game virtual rewards after watching an ad.

Google AdMob may collect and process data such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information according to Google's own policies. This data may be used by Google to provide, limit, measure, and improve advertising services.

4. Consent

For users in regions where consent is required, ValoCase may use Google's User Messaging Platform to request, manage, and store advertising-related consent choices. Users may be shown a consent form depending on their region and applicable law.

5. In-App Purchases

ValoCase may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Payments and purchase processing are handled by Google Play. ValoCase does not directly collect or store full payment card details or banking information.

Purchase-related information, such as purchased virtual currency or virtual items, may be stored by ValoCase to deliver purchased content, restore purchases where applicable, prevent abuse, and provide support.

6. Third-Party Services

ValoCase may use third-party services that process data according to their own privacy policies. These services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Google Play services
  • Unity services
  • Backend hosting and database providers

These third-party services may collect, process, or store data independently according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Data Sharing

ValoCase does not sell users' personal data. Data may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the game, provide advertisements, process purchases, host backend services, analyze technical issues, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Storage and Security

ValoCase uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect gameplay data and related information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

ValoCase keeps gameplay-related data for as long as necessary to provide the game, maintain user progress, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms. Some data may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed.

10. Children's Privacy

ValoCase is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal data to ValoCase, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. User Choices and Data Requests

Users may contact us to request information about their data, request correction, or request deletion where applicable by law. Some requests may require verification to protect user accounts and prevent unauthorized access.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
