ValoCase Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

ValoCase is a mobile game that uses virtual currency and virtual items. Virtual currency, skins, rewards, and other in-game items have no real-world monetary value and cannot be cashed out, sold, or exchanged for real money.

Data We Collect

ValoCase may collect and process anonymous gameplay data such as guest account identifier, wallet balance, inventory, mission progress, battle results, rewarded ad status, and related gameplay activity. This data is used to provide game features, synchronize progress, prevent abuse, and improve the game experience.

Advertising

ValoCase uses Google AdMob to show rewarded advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process data such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and other data according to Google's policies.

Consent

For users in regions where consent is required, ValoCase uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage consent for advertising-related data processing.

Third-Party Services

ValoCase may use third-party services including Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Unity services, and backend hosting providers. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Children

ValoCase is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
